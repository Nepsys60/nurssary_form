Agronomist Recommended Ratio

The Kathmandu Rooftop Potting Formula

Kathmandu terrace containers face intense heat in dry months and torrential monsoon rain in summer. A standard garden soil alone turns like cement. Use this balanced ratio to keep soil loose, rich, and well-aerated.

50%
Local Soil (Garden or Red Soil)
Provides structural anchor and essential native mineral base.
30%
Kinnau Cow Dung or Vermicompost
Acts as a living moisture sponge, supplying continuous humic nutrition.
15%
Low-EC Washed Cocopeat
Drastically reduces structural weight on concrete slabs and aerates roots.
5%
Neem Khali & Bone Meal
Protects against subterranean white grubs and promotes prolific blooms.
Monsoon Drainage Rule

During heavy July–August rains, poorly aerated pot soil waterlogs and rots roots. Cocopeat combined with coarse vermicompost ensures excess water drains quickly through pot drainage holes while retaining beneficial microbes.

Monthly Top-Dressing Routine

Rooftop pots lose nutrients with regular watering. Scratch 2–3 handfuls of pure Kinnau Vermicompost into the top 2 inches of soil once every 3 weeks to keep plants lush without repotting.

Container Gardening Realities

Why Chemical Fertilizers Fail in Kathmandu Pots

In an open farm, chemicals leach into the ground. But in a rooftop container or pot, chemical salts cannot escape. Here is what happens inside your pots.

Chemical Fertilizers (Urea / DAP)

  • ✕ Toxic Salt Buildup: Excess chemical salts accumulate at the bottom of pots, causing brown scorched leaf margins and root dieback.
  • ✕ Hard Brick Compaction: Synthetic nitrogen sterilizes beneficial worms and bacteria, turning potting soil rock-hard when dry.
  • ✕ Short-Lived Spurt: Gives an artificial green rush for 10 days followed by sudden plant exhaustion and blossom abortion.

Kinnau Cured Organic Compost

  • ✓ Living Soil Biology: Packed with active mycorrhizal fungi and earthworm castings that break down minerals into plant-ready food.
  • ✓ Natural Sponge Structure: Humic organic matter retains up to 40% more moisture, protecting plants when you miss a daily watering.
  • ✓ Zero Root Burn Risk: Slow, continuous microbial release feeds plants safely regardless of season or container size.
